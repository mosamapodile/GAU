# GAU

### Money, together.

**GAU is group-native financial infrastructure designed to make managing money together simpler, safer, and more transparent.**

Instead of treating shared money like an individual bank account with multiple people attached, GAU is built around the group itself.

---

## The Problem

Millions of people already manage money together through:

- Stokvels
- Families
- Savings groups
- Friends
- Clubs
- Communities
- Investment groups
- Small organizations

But managing that money often means combining:

**WhatsApp + bank transfers + screenshots + spreadsheets + manual administration + trust.**

This creates simple but expensive problems:

- Who has paid?
- Who still owes money?
- Where did the money go?
- Who approved a withdrawal?
- Who is allowed to spend?
- How should payouts be distributed?
- Can everyone see the same financial history?

GAU aims to solve this.

---
